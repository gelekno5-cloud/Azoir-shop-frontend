Gold, platinum or silver

Choosing the metal.

If you'll wear your signet every day, the metal matters as much as the design. Here is how the options compare.

Gold, 10K to 24K

In yellow, white or rose. 10K and 14K are the most durable golds for a ring that takes daily wear. 18K brings a warmer, richer color. 22K and 24K are the deepest and softest, best for a ring worn gently.

Platinum

Dense, naturally white and hypoallergenic. Over time it develops a soft matte patina, and it can be polished bright again whenever you like.

Sterling silver

An accessible choice with a bright, cool shine. It's softer than gold and tarnishes gently with air; a quick polish restores it.

Plain metal, or set with a stone: a natural diamond, a colored gemstone or a stone of your own. You approve every stone before it's set. More in our materials guide.

Good to know

Before you begin.

How much does a custom signet ring cost?

Because every piece is one of a kind, there's no set price list. Once your design is defined, we send a detailed quote, so you know the full cost before committing to production. Your budget is part of the conversation from the very start; we design to it, not around it.

What is the $60 design fee?

It's paid when you send your request, and it reserves your designer's time to create your three concepts and 3D render. The full $60 is credited toward your ring's final price. Because it pays for design work, it isn't refundable. After that, payment comes in clear milestones: a deposit before production and the balance before delivery.

Do I need to be in New York?

No. Meet us in person in New York, or by video from anywhere. We craft every piece in New York and ship your finished ring to you, fully insured.

How long does it take?

It depends on the design. Most commissions take a few weeks from approved design to delivery, and your exact timeline is shared with your quote. If you're working toward a date, tell us early and we'll plan around it.

What if I don't know my ring size?

Rings are made to the size you confirm before production. Our ring size guide shows simple ways to measure at home, and your designer can help.

Can I use gold or a stone I already have?

Yes. We can rework inherited metal or reset an existing stone while keeping the story intact. Mention it when you tell us about your signet.
