# Azoir & Co. — Off-Site SEO Playbook

The on-site work (landing pages, schema, journal, sitemap) is done in code.
Everything below needs a human with account access — none of it can be automated.
Ordered by impact.

## 1. Google Business Profile — do this first

For searches like "custom jeweler NYC" the **map pack ranks above every organic
result**, and a new business can appear there within weeks.

1. Go to https://business.google.com and create the profile with the same name
   everywhere: **Azoir & Co.**
2. Category: **Jewelry designer** (primary), **Jeweler** (secondary).
3. If the atelier address shouldn't be public, choose a **service-area business**
   (New York) — you still rank in the map pack.
4. Add: hello@azoir.co, azoir.co, hours, and 10+ photos of real pieces
   (the gallery and Instagram content are perfect).
5. Post monthly (a finished commission works as a post).

## 2. Reviews — the map-pack ranking fuel

- After every delivered commission, send the review link from the Business
  Profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews" button gives a short URL).
- One line in the delivery email: *"If the ring made you smile, two sentences
  on Google mean the world to a small atelier."*
- Reply to every review. Never buy or fake reviews — Google detects it and
  suspends profiles.

## 3. Free high-authority listings (backlinks + leads)

Each is a profile only the owner can create:

| Where | URL | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Knot vendor profile | https://www.theknot.com/vendor-signup | Wedding traffic + strong backlink |
| WeddingWire | https://www.weddingwire.com/vendors | Same network, second listing |
| Yelp for Business | https://biz.yelp.com | NYC local searches |
| Bing Places | https://www.bingplaces.com | Can import from Google Business Profile |
| Apple Business Connect | https://businessconnect.apple.com | Apple Maps |
| Pinterest business account | https://business.pinterest.com | Jewelry is a top Pinterest category; pin gallery pieces linking to azoir.co |

Keep name / email / city identical on every listing.

## 4. Content cadence — two journal articles a month

The journal exists at `/journal/`. Next titles, in priority order (each targets
a real search with weak competition):

1. How much does it cost to reset a diamond ring?
2. Custom wedding bands: how pricing by gram actually works
3. What to expect at a custom jewelry consultation
4. Signet rings: how to choose a style, metal and engraving
5. Toi et moi rings: the two-stone engagement ring, explained
6. Ring sizes: how to secretly find out theirs
7. Sapphire engagement rings: colors, durability, and cost
8. How CAD rendering works in custom jewelry (show the real pipeline)
9. Matte, brushed or polished: choosing a finish that lasts
10. How to insure a custom engagement ring

House rules for every article: same template as the three live ones (Article +
FAQPage schema, `.legal` prose, visible FAQ section, one CTA at the end,
absolute links, added to `sitemap.xml`).

## 5. Press and expert quotes

- Sign up at **Qwoted** (https://www.qwoted.com) and answer journalist requests
  about jewelry, weddings and diamonds — each answer can become a quote + link
  in Brides, The Zoe Report, NYT gift guides, etc.
- Pitch NYC-local: Time Out New York gift guides, New York Magazine weddings,
  Brooklyn/Manhattan neighborhood blogs. The hook is the story: one-designer
  atelier, hand sketches, 3D renders, heirloom redesigns.

## 6. Social → search

Instagram (@azoir.nyc) and TikTok (@azoir.co) are already linked in the site's
schema. For search impact: put **azoir.co/journal links in bios and pinned
posts**, and turn each journal article into a carousel/short — search engines
increasingly surface TikTok/IG for "custom ring" queries.

## 7. Measure monthly

Search Console property `sc-domain:azoir.co` is verified and the sitemap is
submitted. Once impressions grow (give it 4–6 weeks):

- Top queries report → which articles are getting impressions
- Quick wins: queries at position 4–10 with low CTR → improve those pages'
  titles or add a section
- Watch "Pages" indexing report — all 21 pages should index within a few weeks

Ask Claude in the admin workspace for these reports — the GSC connection is
already set up.
