Before you measure

  • Measure at the end of the day, when your fingers are at their warmest and largest.
  • Cold hands measure small. Avoid measuring when you're cold or just out of the water.
  • Measure the finger the ring will actually be worn on — hands differ.
  • If your knuckle is wider than the base of your finger, size to slide comfortably over the knuckle.
  • Wider bands fit more snugly — if you're choosing a wide band, consider going up a quarter to half size.

Method 1 — measure a ring you already own

Take a ring that already fits the correct finger, and measure the distance straight across the inside of the band in millimeters. Match that inner diameter to the chart above. This is the most reliable method.

Method 2 — measure your finger

Wrap a thin strip of paper or a piece of string snugly around the base of your finger. Mark where it overlaps, lay it flat, and measure the length in millimeters — that's your circumference. Match it to the chart above.

Common questions

What ring size is 17 mm?

A ring that measures 17 mm straight across the inside is about a US 6½ (UK M½, EU 53). A US 6¾ is 17.1 mm, so if your ring measures a little over 17 mm, choose the 6¾.

How many mm is a size 6½ ring?

A US 6½ is 16.9 mm across the inside and 53.2 mm around (2.09 in). That's about a UK M½ or EU 53.

What ring size is 2.5 inches around?

A finger that measures 2.5 in around (63.5 mm) is about a US 10½ (UK U½, EU 63).

What ring size is 5.5 cm?

5.5 cm around the finger (55 mm) is about a US 7¼ (UK O, EU 55).

What US size is EU ring size 50, 58 or 60?

EU sizes follow the ISO standard: the number is the inside circumference in millimeters. EU 50 is about a US 5¼, EU 58 about a US 8½ and EU 60 about a US 9¼.

How do I find my thumb ring size?

Measure it the same way as any finger: around the base of the thumb, or across the inside of a thumb ring that fits. Thumb knuckles are wide, so make sure the size will slide over yours.
